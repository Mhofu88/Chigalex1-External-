INTRODUCTION — WHAT IS PI NETWORK?

Pi Network is a global digital currency and ecosystem project designed to make cryptocurrency accessible and useful to everyday people.

Unlike traditional cryptocurrencies that often require expensive mining machines and high electricity consumption, Pi Network allows ordinary people to participate directly from their mobile phones.

The vision of Pi Network is to build a people-powered ecosystem where digital currency can be used for real utility, real services, real communities and real economic participation.

The Global Mission of Pi Network

  • Build an inclusive digital economy
  • Empower ordinary people globally
  • Increase real-world utility adoption
  • Support peer-to-peer transactions
  • Build a decentralized ecosystem
  • Encourage community participation
