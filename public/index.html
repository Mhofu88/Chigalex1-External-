INTRODUCTION — WHAT IS PI NETWORK?

Pi Network is a digital currency and global ecosystem project designed to make cryptocurrency accessible and useful for everyone.

Unlike traditional cryptocurrencies that require expensive mining machines, Pi Network allows ordinary people to participate directly from their phones.

Why Pi for Africa?

  • Build an inclusive digital economy without banks
  • Empower ordinary people worldwide with mobile mining
  • Increase real-life usage through utilities and DApps
  • Support person-to-person commerce and reduce fees
  • Created by Stanford PhDs - Dr. Nicolas Kokkalis & Dr. Chengdiao Fan
  • Over 60 Million Pioneers worldwide

CHAPTER 2 — PI WALLET EDUCATION

Your Pi Wallet is your bank in the Pi Ecosystem. It holds your Pi coins securely on the blockchain.

How to create: Open Pi Browser → Wallet → Generate Wallet → COPY your 24 words → Save offline in 2 safe places.

Receiving Pi: Share your public address (starts with G...). Sending Pi: Paste receiver address, enter amount, confirm.

Wallet Safety Rules:

  • Write passphrase on paper, not screenshot
  • Store in 2 different safe places (not phone only)
  • Test with 1 Pi before sending large amount
  • Check first 4 and last 4 letters of address always

CHAPTER 5 — UNDERSTANDING GCV

Global Consensus Value (GCV) encourages real utility. 1 Pi = $314,159 GCV. This value is built by pioneers using Pi in real trade, not by exchange price.

GCV Meaning: GCV is not the exchange price. It is a movement to protect Pi's value by using Pi at a high consensus in our own barter. When we use Pi for real goods at GCV, we create demand and utility. This is economic empowerment for Africa.
